Installation guide

Install JEETrack PW Sync

Set up the extension in 4 simple steps. It takes about two minutes and works on Chrome and Edge.

1

Download the extension ZIP

Save the PW Sync ZIP to your computer. Keep it zipped — no need to unzip.

Download ZIP
2

Open Extensions & enable Developer Mode

Open your browser’s extensions page, then switch on Developer mode (top-right corner).

Browsers don’t let websites open this page directly, so we copy chrome://extensions — paste it in a new tab.
3

Drag the ZIP onto the page

Open your downloads (click the download icon in the toolbar, or press Ctrl + J), then drag the ZIP directly onto the Extensions page. The browser installs PW Sync for you.

4

Connect & sync

Open a supported PW test result page. A JEETrack bar appears at the top. If it asks you to log in, click Open JEETrack and sign in. Then press Send to JEETrack.

Optional: tick Analyse mistakes to also send your questions to Optima for analysis.
